SPACE WARS — PHASE 2 LOCAL MATCH PROTOTYPE
8-PLAYER QUICK CONQUEST / FULL PLAYABLE LOCAL MATCH

We have validated the visual direction of the current Space Wars prototype.

The existing dashboard and galaxy map are GOOD ENOUGH that I want to continue developing the game.

Do NOT throw away the current prototype and rebuild it from scratch unless a specific subsystem genuinely requires replacement.

Build on what already works.

The purpose of this phase is to answer a new question:

"Is Space Wars actually FUN as a fast 8-player free-for-all strategy match?"

This is still a LOCAL-ONLY gameplay prototype.

We are NOT building the production multiplayer backend yet.

We are NOT implementing Supabase yet.

We are NOT implementing accounts yet.

We are going to turn the current visual prototype into a real, complete, locally playable match:

1 human player
+
7 local AI commanders

The player should be able to play from opening expansion through wars and objectives to an actual victory/defeat screen.

==================================================
IMPORTANT CHANGE IN PRODUCT DIRECTION
==================================================

We now see two possible future games in this universe.

A future persistent "Campaign" game may last days/weeks and use deeper combat, diplomacy and persistent progression.

THIS PROTOTYPE IS NOT THAT GAME.

For now we are building the fast version:

SPACE WARS — QUICK CONQUEST

Working product concept:

An 8-player free-for-all browser strategy match lasting approximately 35–45 minutes.

Every commander starts from an equal home system.

Players rapidly:

- expand through nearby star systems
- capture planets
- mine asteroid fields
- construct fleets
- fight pirates
- contest strategic systems
- fight the other seven commanders
- compete for special objectives
- react to large neutral threats
- accumulate Dominion Score
- attempt to control the galaxy before time expires

The game should feel fast and active but NOT like an APM/click-spam RTS.

I do NOT want:

click click click click 50 times per second.

I DO want:

one meaningful strategic decision every roughly 10–30 seconds while previously issued orders continue executing automatically.

The player should frequently be thinking:

- Where do I expand?
- What resource do I need?
- Do I fight these pirates?
- Do I contest that strategic system?
- Should I attack this weakened commander?
- Should I reinforce my border?
- Is that objective worth the risk?
- Do I build more cheap ships or save for heavy ships?
- Do I retreat?
- Can I steal an objective while two rivals are fighting?

GALAXY SIZE
==================================================

The old 140–180 system visual prototype is too large for this match format.

Create a compact but strategically meaningful match galaxy.

Initial target:

24–32 STAR SYSTEMS

Recommended starting point:

28 systems

8 commanders means approximately:

3–4 meaningful systems per commander plus shared/core objectives.

Each system can contain multiple internal objects.

without requiring 100 strategic nodes.

This should create enough room to expand while bringing players into conflict reasonably quickly.

GALAXY LAYOUT
==================================================

Generate the galaxy deterministically from a seed.

Structure approximately:

OUTER REGION
- 8 starting home systems
- safer initial expansion
- basic resource opportunities

MID REGION
- contested neutral systems
- better resources
- pirates
- strategic bonuses
- first major player collisions

CORE REGION

- major score opportunities

==================================================
PLAYER SPAWNS
==================================================

Place eight home systems approximately evenly around the galaxy.

Requirements:

- comparable early expansion opportunities
- comparable access to basic resources
- multiple possible expansion routes
- no player spawning immediately trapped
- no player receiving obviously dominant starting territory

The human gets one spawn.

Seven bots receive the others.
